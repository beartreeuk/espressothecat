About

From December 2025 to October 2026 I worked operations at a UK vehicle-leasing broker: the internal ticket queue, order admin for nine salespeople, hands-on IT, and from August the front end of the company’s new website.

A back office is full of work that repeats, so alongside the day job I built tools to take it over.

What I did

  • A cancellation system. Cancelling an order became one recorded process instead of a chain of manual steps.
  • An invoicing bridge. Invoices reached the accounts software without being retyped.
  • An image uploader. Marketing put images on the website themselves instead of raising a ticket.
  • An order integration with a manufacturer’s leasing platform. Nine salespeople placed 215 orders through it. I administered those orders, reconciled 176 of them against the dealer system, and was the main contact for the integration partner on testing and API errors.
  • Around the tools, the ticket queue: 224 of the 226 tickets assigned to me closed.
